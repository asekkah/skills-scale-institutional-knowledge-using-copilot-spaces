# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. It includes both core delivery roles and supporting roles that strengthen accountability, communication, and project outcomes.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness, deployment planning, and post-release validation. They help teams deliver updates with clear communication and controlled rollback procedures.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release schedules and coordinate with product and engineering teams
- Maintain release checklists, deployment runbooks, and rollback plans
- Confirm release readiness using quality and operational criteria
- Monitor production deployment success and communicate outcomes
- Coordinate release communications with stakeholders and support teams

### Interacts with
- Works closely with Project Managers to align milestones and launch windows
- Coordinates with Developers and QA Leads on release quality gates
- Partner with Product Managers to confirm customer impact and communication timing
- Reports deployment status and issues to Stakeholder Managers and leadership

### Goals
- Deliver predictable, low-risk releases
- Reduce deployment surprises and recovery effort
- Maintain clear release communication throughout the launch process

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and go/no-go discussions
- Deployment checklists and incident updates
- Status summaries for stakeholders and support teams

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical direction for solution design, platform decisions, and long-term maintainability. They help the team balance speed, scalability, security, and architectural quality.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs for scalability, performance, and maintainability
- Guide technology choices, patterns, and integration strategies
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and architectural constraints early
- Support Developers in solving complex technical problems
- Align engineering decisions with business goals and platform standards

### Interacts with
- Advises Developers on implementation approach and design trade-offs
- Partners with Product Managers on feasibility and scope realism
- Supports Project Managers with risks, dependencies, and technical planning
- Collaborates with Security Champions on secure architecture decisions

### Goals
- Maintain system quality and long-term technical health
- Reduce avoidable rework caused by architecture drift
- Improve alignment between product needs and engineering constraints

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Technical risk assessments and dependency mapping
- Key design decisions and standards guidance

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Coaches improve team effectiveness by facilitating agile practices, removing blockers, and supporting continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove or escalate blockers that slow delivery
- Coach teams on agile practices, rituals, and working agreements
- Support healthy collaboration and continuous improvement habits
- Help maintain a sustainable pace and transparent workflow

### Interacts with
- Works with Project Managers to keep delivery flow and stakeholder expectations aligned
- Supports Developers and Product Managers in prioritization and decision making
- Helps cross-functional teams resolve conflict and improve process clarity
- Partners with Stakeholder Managers to communicate team health and delivery trends

### Goals
- Improve team velocity without sacrificing quality
- Create a predictable, healthy delivery cadence
- Strengthen collaboration, learning, and accountability across the team

### Typical Communication
- Team ceremonies and facilitation
- Retrospective action tracking
- Team health and delivery rhythm updates

---

## Stakeholder Manager / Communications Lead

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers or Communications Leads keep leaders, sponsors, and partner teams informed and aligned. They translate project status into understandable updates and ensure stakeholder needs are addressed.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain stakeholder maps and engagement plans
- Communicate progress, risks, and decisions to leadership and partner teams
- Coordinate status updates, change communications, and escalation pathways
- Ensure stakeholder feedback is incorporated into project decisions when appropriate
- Support relationship management across business and technical groups

### Interacts with
- Works with Project Managers to align messaging and reporting cadence
- Partner with Product Managers to communicate roadmap and trade-offs
- Coordinates with Release Managers on go-live messaging and deployment updates
- Provides context to Developers and team leads on stakeholder concerns and expectations

### Goals
- Maintain trust and alignment across stakeholders
- Reduce confusion caused by uneven communication
- Ensure project decisions are informed by stakeholder needs

### Typical Communication
- Executive updates and stakeholder briefings
- Decision summaries and risk communications
- Cross-functional alignment meetings and change notices

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts connect business needs and delivery teams by clarifying requirements, validating assumptions, and helping teams turn ideas into actionable work.

### Responsibilities
- Capture and refine business requirements, workflows, and user needs
- Translate stakeholder input into user stories, acceptance criteria, and process definitions
- Identify gaps, dependencies, and operational impacts during planning
- Support solution validation by checking whether delivered work meets business intent
- Document processes and decision context for future teams

### Interacts with
- Collaborates with Product Managers to refine problem statements and priorities
- Works with Developers and QA Leads to validate requirements and acceptance criteria
- Helps Project Managers understand dependencies and operational impacts
- Supports Stakeholder Managers by clarifying business context and trade-offs

### Goals
- Improve requirement clarity and reduce ambiguity
- Align business outcomes with delivery work
- Strengthen continuity between stakeholder expectations and implementation

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and process reviews
- User stories, acceptance criteria, and clarifying questions
- Cross-functional requirements alignment discussions

---

## Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Champions help embed security awareness and controls throughout the project lifecycle. They reduce risk by ensuring security is considered during design, implementation, and release.

### Responsibilities
- Review plans, designs, and implementations for security risks
- Help define security requirements and secure coding expectations
- Coordinate with QA teams on security testing and validation
- Support compliance, incident response readiness, and risk mitigation planning
- Educate the team on secure practices and known vulnerabilities

### Interacts with
- Partners with Product Managers to define security requirements and risk tolerance
- Works with Developers and Technical Architects to assess design and implementation choices
- Supports Project Managers in identifying security-related risks and dependencies
- Collaborates with Release Managers to validate security readiness before deployment

### Goals
- Reduce security risk before issues reach production
- Improve secure-by-default implementation practices
- Strengthen trust in project delivery and release confidence

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and design discussions
- Risk assessments and remediation planning
- Release readiness feedback and security validation notes

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The expanded set helps teams define accountability clearly across delivery, stakeholder management, security, and operations.
- Cross-functional roles should be paired with core roles to improve clarity in planning, execution, communication, and continuous improvement.
